# SailZone usage (one page)

**Brand:** SailZone · **Tagline:** Race the rules.

## Clear space

- **Full logo:** leave empty margin at least the **height of the yellow mark** on all sides.
- **Icon only:** leave empty margin at least **half the icon height** on all sides.

## Minimum sizes

| Asset | Digital | Print |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Full logo | ≥ **120 px** wide | ≥ **25 mm** wide |
| Icon | ≥ **24 px** | — |

## Preferred placements

1. **Color** lockup on white / light backgrounds (default).
2. **White mono** (`#FFFFFF`) on dark navy (`#02285C` or `#0B1747`).
3. **Black mono** (`#0B1747`) on light when color isn’t available.

## Don’ts

- Don’t recolor the boats independently (green = starboard, red = port).
- Don’t remove the dashed three-boat-length zone circle.
- Don’t stretch, skew, or rotate the lockup off-axis.
- Don’t add drop shadows, outlines, or glow effects to the mark.
- Don’t place the logo on busy photos without a solid plate (white or navy).
