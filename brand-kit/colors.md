# SailZone colors

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Yellow mark | `#FFC400` | Mark (circle) and three-boat-length zone dash |
| Green starboard | `#18A15E` | Starboard boat (RRS starboard) |
| Red port | `#E1182B` | Port boat (RRS port) |
| Navy wordmark | `#02285C` | SAILZONE wordmark; dark UI / OG navy option |
| Circle / navy dark | `#0B1747` | Branded mono black; deep navy plates |
| Laylines | `#22808D` | Course layline strokes |
| Tagline | `#177F8D` | “Race the rules.” |

## Notes

- Green / red are not decorative only—they encode **Racing Rules of Sailing** starboard / port.
- Prefer full color on white or light backgrounds.
- When color is unavailable: black mono (`#0B1747`) on light; white mono (`#FFFFFF`) on `#02285C` or `#0B1747`.
- Do not invent alternate boat colors or recolor boats independently of the lockup.
